What this is

Three principals — Alice, Bob, Carol — each in their own fumola space. Alice and Bob each publish their own per-day acceptance policy as a fumola thunk; Carol composes them and runs a short-circuiting search for the first feasible day. Each round is a different snapshot of policies. The full source of the handler is one fumola module; the runtime container loads it once and invokes handle(method, path, body) on every request.

Why the digest is the point

Every response includes X-Fumola-DCG-Digest — a sha256 over (project, method, path, body, output). Because the program is restricted to deterministic prims (no clocks, no RNG, no I/O), two requests with the same inputs always produce byte-identical outputs and therefore identical digests. In an attested deployment, the dstack TEE quote signs over the digest; any client can replay the request and verify by hash equality alone.

The narrative across the four rounds

  • v1: standard cap-only policies on both sides. Tuesday fits.
  • v2: Bob amends his published policy to refuse Tuesdays. The search has to look further; Thursday is the new first feasible day.
  • v3: Alice's calendar fills up — every day is at her cap. With Bob's Tuesday block still in effect, no day fits. The receipt is a complete failure log.
  • v4: Alice negotiates by raising her own cap from 8 to 10. She doesn't change anyone else's policy, doesn't unblock Bob's Tuesday — just amends her own bundle. Thursday becomes feasible again.
